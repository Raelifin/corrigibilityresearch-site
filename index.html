The Corrigibility Research Fund A program of Lightcone Infrastructure

Funding research on AI systems that help us get everything right the first time notice and correct our mistakes.

The Corrigibility Research Fund awards grants and prizes for research that advances our understanding of AI corrigibility. In 2026 the fund is disbursing at least $200,000, split roughly evenly between the two.

Grants
Apply on Lightcone Commons and mention corrigibility. Typical grants are $5k–$35k. Applications are treated as public; early submissions get more feedback.

Corrigibility, n. The property of an (AI) agent that keeps its (human) principal informed and in control — one that helps us notice and fix the flaws in its thoughts, structure, and actions. Corrigibility moves the prospect of building superhuman AI towards an iterative process with room for error.
